About the Summer School

The Society of Young Scientists in Biophysics Summer School (often called Natsugaku) is an approximately four-day residential study camp for undergraduate students, graduate students, and early-career researchers from across Japan who major in biophysics or are interested in the field.

It has a long history, dating back to 1960, when the late Dr. Fumio Oosawa first held the school even before the founding of its parent society, the Biophysical Society of Japan. Since then, it has been planned and run each year, over roughly a year of preparation, by volunteer undergraduate and graduate students.

The summer school is an excellent opportunity to engage with a wide range of topics in biophysics, deepening participants' knowledge and offering new perspectives through lectures, discussions, and interaction among attendees. It also serves as an important venue for building connections across disciplines.

For the latest information on the summer school, please see the official website of The Society of Young Scientists in Biophysics.

Research Exchange Seminar: Research Introductions and Career Choices

June 30, 2023

We held a research exchange seminar at Enyu Gakusha on the Hokkaido University campus. We invited Prof. Kita (Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences), Prof. Yamauchi (Institute of Low Temperature Science), and Ms. Nakamura (Graduate School of Life Science) — all active researchers at Hokkaido University — to speak about their research and their career choices. With 57 attendees in total, the event sparked lively discussion on topics such as pursuing a PhD and building a research career in academia.

Fellowship Seminar: Financial Support for Graduate Students

May 27, 2022

In recent years, more funding opportunities have become available to graduate students, but for those considering applying, the details of each program and the workload involved can be hard to grasp. For this seminar, we invited students who had actually been awarded fellowships to speak in detail about what the experience is really like.
