The Society of Young Scientists in Biophysics

I have taken part in The Society of Young Scientists in Biophysics since my undergraduate years, directing the 64th Summer School in 2024 and serving as national chair of the steering committee in FY2025. I continue to support the early-career research community by organizing the summer school and branch seminars and contributing to the bulletin Seibutsu Butsuri.

Science writing & communication

In FY2025 (2025–2026), I took part in Hokkaido University’s CoSTEP program and wrote for its web magazine, いいね！Hokudai. My reporting covered researchers, graduate student life, the university festival, student groups, and Sapporo’s cafe culture.
