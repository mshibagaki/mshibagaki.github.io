HPLC Analyzer

Research desktop app · In development

A desktop app for comparing and analyzing chromatography runs in one place. It imports data from Shimadzu chromatography software and overlays multiple chromatograms for comparison. It also supports peak integration, quantification, and figure or report export while preserving the original measurements.
