Producing soluble antimicrobial peptides and probing structure and interactions

I develop methods to produce aggregation-prone antimicrobial peptides in soluble form and use NMR spectroscopy and molecular dynamics simulations to study fusion protein structures and antimicrobial peptide interactions.

Research themes
