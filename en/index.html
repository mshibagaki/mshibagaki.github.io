研究

Research

Focusing on the antimicrobial peptides that underpin host defense, I aim to understand the relationship between their structure and function at the molecular level.

Recombinant production of hard-to-express peptides

Developing methods based on a calmodulin fusion tag to produce aggregation- and degradation-prone peptides in the soluble fraction.

Structural properties and interactions of antimicrobial peptides

Investigating how oligomerization, membrane interactions, and structural differences among closely related orthologs relate to the function of antimicrobial peptides such as LL-37.

Structure and dynamics by NMR and MD

Capturing the structures and motions that peptides adopt in solution, and relating them to their physiological functions.

2024–2027 / Grant-in-Aid for JSPS Fellows

Structural and interaction analysis of antimicrobial peptides, molecules that drive inflammation and homeostasis in the brain and intestine
